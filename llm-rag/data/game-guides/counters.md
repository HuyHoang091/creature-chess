# Counter Strategies - Creature Chess

## Counter Matrix

### vs Fire-heavy teams
- **Use**: Water carries (Nudikill, Eaglace) + Earth tanks
- **Formation**: Spread out to avoid AoE Flame Burst damage
- **Items**: Giant's Belt / Warmog on frontline for HP to survive bursts

### vs Earth-heavy teams (tanky frontline)
- **Use**: Cunning carries (fast DPS to burst through defense)
- **Strategy**: Focus fire with Assassin Flank formation
- **Items**: Infinity Edge, B.F. Sword for maximum damage

### vs Wood-heavy teams (sustain/healing)
- **Use**: Fire AoE (Agnigon, Cardinale) to out-damage healing
- **Strategy**: Clump your carries to focus damage on one target at a time
- **Items**: Rabadon (damage + mana for faster skill casts)

### vs Metal-heavy teams (piercing)
- **Use**: Valiant tanks (high defense + HP to absorb piercing)
- **Strategy**: Tank Front formation with multiple frontline layers
- **Items**: Chain Vest, Thornmail to reflect damage

### vs Cunning/Assassin teams
- **Use**: Anti-jump formation (corners + edges)
- **Strategy**: Place carry in corner, surround with tanks/supports
- **Items**: Phantom Dancer on carry (dodge), Guardian Angel (revive)

## Positional Counters

### Enemy clumped together
- **Counter**: AoE damage (Flame Burst, Earthquake)
- **Formation**: Spread your units, focus AoE on enemy cluster

### Enemy spread out
- **Counter**: Single-target burst (Metal Pierce, Laser Beam)
- **Formation**: Focus Corner to concentrate your damage

### Enemy has strong backline carry
- **Counter**: Assassin Flank formation to dive enemy carry
- **Place fast cunning units on edges to bypass frontline**

### Enemy has assassins
- **Counter**: Anti-jump formation, carry in corner
- **Use tanks as bodyguards adjacent to carry
