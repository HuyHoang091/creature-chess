# Build Guides - Creature Chess

## Element Synergies

### Fire (fire)
- **Creatures**: Embra(1), Cardiling(2), Agnite(2), Ignibus(3), Ruption(3), Prophetoise(4), Tikorch(4), Agnigon(5), Cardinale(5)
- **Strengths**: High AoE damage (Flame Burst, Fire AoE skills), good for clearing clumped formations
- **Best carries**: Agnigon (cost 5, valiant — tanky + high damage), Cardinale (cost 5, cunning — fast)
- **Pairs well with**: Metal (tank frontline), Water (backline coverage)

### Water (water)
- **Creatures**: Tweesher(1), Elowind(2), Fluttaflap(2), Noctalo(3), Lightmare(3), Nudimind(4), Dollfin(4), Nudikill(5), Eaglace(5)
- **Strengths**: Chain Lightning bounces, good sustain with Dollfin's heal
- **Best carries**: Nudikill (cost 5, valiant), Eaglace (cost 5, cunning — fast)
- **Pairs well with**: Wood (healing support), Fire (damage)

### Earth (earth)
- **Creatures**: Rockitten(1), Aardorn(1), Chenipode(2), Grintot(3), Rockat(3), Aardart(4), Hubursa(4), Grintrock(5), Jemuar(5)
- **Strengths**: Earthquake AoE, high HP/defense, great frontline
- **Best tanks**: Grintrock (cost 5, valiant), Grintot (cost 3, valiant)
- **Pairs well with**: Metal (double frontline), Fire (damage behind)

### Wood (wood)
- **Creatures**: Budaye(1), Anoleaf(1), Bamboon(2), Velocitile(3), Sapsnap(3), Narcileaf(4), Coleorus(4), Arbelder(5), Viviphyta(5)
- **Strengths**: Healing (Nature Blessing, Mend), sustain, outlast opponents
- **Best supports**: Arbelder (cost 5, valiant — healer + tank), Narcileaf (cost 4, arcane — ranged healer)
- **Pairs well with**: Earth (tanky + sustain), Metal (protect healers)

### Metal (metal)
- **Creatures**: Nut(1), Puparmor(1), Weavifly(1), Bolt(2), Propellorcat(3), Sumchon(3), Cairfrey(3), Sampsack(4), Pyraminx(5), AV8R(5), Kirkanon(5)
- **Strengths**: Piercing damage (Laser Beam, Fatal Thrust), strong carries
- **Best carries**: AV8R (cost 5, cunning — fast piercing), Kirkanon (cost 5, arcane — ranged piercing)
- **Pairs well with**: Earth (frontline), Wood (sustain)

## Combat Trait Roles

### Valiant
- **Role**: Tank / Bruiser
- **Stats**: High HP (0.4), high Defense (0.3), moderate Attack (0.2), moderate Speed (0.2)
- **Attack type**: Melee (basic, range 1)
- **Best for**: Frontline, soaking damage

### Arcane
- **Role**: Ranged Carry / Mage
- **Stats**: High Attack (0.4), high Speed (0.3), moderate HP/Defense
- **Attack type**: Ranged (shoot, range 2)
- **Best for**: Backline damage dealer

### Cunning
- **Role**: Assassin / DPS
- **Stats**: Highest Attack (0.4), highest Speed (0.45), low HP/Defense
- **Attack type**: Melee (basic, range 1)
- **Best for**: Fast burst damage, flanking

## Recommended Team Compositions

### 1. Fire + Metal (Aggressive)
- **Core**: Agnigon, AV8R, Pyraminx, Cardinale
- **Frontline**: Grintrock or Sampsack
- **Why**: High burst damage + piercing, overwhelm opponents before they can heal

### 2. Earth + Wood (Sustain)
- **Core**: Grintrock, Arbelder, Narcileaf, Aardart
- **Support**: Bamboon, Budaye
- **Why**: Unkillable frontline with constant healing

### 3. Water + Fire (Balanced)
- **Core**: Nudikill, Eaglace, Agnigon, Cardinale
- **Flex**: Grintot (tank), Narcileaf (healer)
- **Why**: AoE damage from both elements, good coverage

### 4. Metal + Cunning (Assassin)
- **Core**: AV8R, Kirkanon, Cardinale, Tikorch
- **Why**: All-in fast damage, kill enemy carry before fight starts
