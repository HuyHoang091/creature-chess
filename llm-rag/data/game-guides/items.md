| Recurve Bow | +15 Speed | Assassins (cunning) |
| Tear of the Goddess | +15 Mana | Skill-reliant units |
| Negatron Cloak | +10 DEF, +10 SPD | Flexible, good on anyone |
| Needlessly Large Rod | +10 ATK, +10 Mana | Mages (arcane) |

## Item Priority by Role

### Tank (Valiant)
1. Warmog's Armor — maximum HP
2. Thornmail — punish melee attackers
3. Frozen Heart — slow enemies + mana for skills

### Carry (Cunning/Arcane)
1. Infinity Edge — maximum damage
2. Bloodthirster — sustain through fights
3. Guardian Angel — insurance against burst

### Support (Wood healers)
1. Tear of the Goddess / Rabadon — faster skill casts (more heals)
2. Warmog — survive long enough to heal team

### Anti-Assassin Carry Build
1. Guardian Angel — revive after assassin burst
2. Phantom Dancer — dodge incoming damage
3. Bloodthirster — heal back up
