# Formation Strategies - Creature Chess

## Board Layout
- Board size: 7 columns (x: 0-6) × 3 rows (y: 0-2) per player
- Row 0 = front row (closest to enemy)
- Row 2 = back row (furthest from enemy)
- Max pieces on board depends on player level

## Formation Templates

### 1. Tank Front
- **When**: You have valiant/earth tanks and ranged carries
- **Layout**: Tanks in row 0, carries in row 2
- **Strengths**: Protects carries, forces enemy to go through frontline
- **Weakness**: Vulnerable to assassin flanking

### 2. Spread Backline
- **When**: Enemy has AoE damage (Fire teams)
- **Layout**: All units spread across row 2, spaced 2 apart
- **Strengths**: Minimizes AoE damage
- **Weakness**: No frontline protection

### 3. Anti-Jump (Anti-Assassin)
- **When**: Enemy has cunning/assassin units
- **Layout**: Carry in corner (0,2 or 6,2), tanks surrounding carry
- **Strengths**: Assassins can't reach carry easily
- **Weakness**: Less damage output due to defensive positioning

### 4. Focus Corner
- **When**: You want concentrated damage on one area
- **Layout**: All units in top-left corner (x: 0-2, y: 0-1)
- **Strengths**: Units support each other, focused fire
- **Weakness**: Vulnerable to AoE, spread-out enemies

### 5. Protect Left/Right
- **When**: You have one strong carry that must survive
- **Layout**: Carry on one side, tanks in front and adjacent
- **Strengths**: Maximum protection for key unit
- **Weakness**: One side of board is empty

### 6. Assassin Flank
- **When**: You have fast cunning units
- **Layout**: Assassins on edges (x: 0 and x: 6), rest in center
- **Strengths**: Assassins bypass frontline to dive enemy carry
- **Weakness**: Assassins may die quickly without support

## Positioning Tips

### General Rules
1. **Always protect your carry** — Place highest DPS unit behind tanks
2. **Tanks go front** — Valiant trait units should always be in row 0
3. **Ranged behind melee** — Arcane (range 2) should be in row 1-2
4. **Spread vs AoE** — If enemy has Fire/Earth AoE, don't clump
5. **Corner carry vs assassins** — If enemy has fast cunning units, use corner

### When to Reposition
- After seeing enemy board in the preview
- When enemy changes their formation
- When you add/remove units from board
- Between rounds to counter what you saw
