# Publisher Information - Creature Chess

## About
- **Game**: Creature Chess
- **Type**: Auto-battler / Auto Chess
- **Platform**: Web-based (browser)
- **Status**: In Development

## Game Overview
Creature Chess is a multiplayer auto-battler where players collect creatures, build teams with elemental synergies, equip items, and position their army on a tactical board. Battles are automatic — strategy comes from team composition, positioning, and item management.

## Core Mechanics
- **47 Creatures** across 5 elements (Fire, Water, Earth, Wood, Metal) and 3 combat traits (Valiant, Arcane, Cunning)
- **3 Stages** per creature (upgrade by combining duplicates)
- **Item System**: 8 base items that combine into 9 powerful tier 2 items
- **Board**: 7×3 grid per player, position your army for battle
- **Economy**: Manage gold for buying, rerolling, and leveling

## Technology
- Built with TypeScript, React, Redux-Saga
- Real-time multiplayer via Socket.IO
- AI opponents powered by reinforcement learning (PPO algorithm)

- For gameplay issues or feedback, use the in-game feedback system.
